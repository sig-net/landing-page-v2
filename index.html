How it works

Your Midnight contract gets its own account on Ethereum.

Sig.Network is a signing network. It gives your contract an account on any chain, and that account only does what your contract tells it to. Ethereum sees an ordinary account doing ordinary things.

  • 01 · Midnight

    Your contract decides.

    Logic, balances and identities stay shielded in your Compact contract.

  • 02 · sig.network

    The network signs.

    A threshold of independent signers produces one ordinary Ethereum signature. Nothing is bridged or wrapped.

  • 03 · Ethereum

    The chain settles.

    One account, one normal transaction, at Uniswap’s or Aave’s real price.

Move the line

Go private one layer at a time.

Put a private pool in front of an app people already use, then move the line down as you go. Everything above it runs on Midnight. Everything below it keeps Ethereum’s depth.

Day one
The same trades, with no leaked data.
Then
The strategy runs on Midnight. Leverage, swaps and liquidations stay where the depth is.
Eventually
Native assets are issued on their chain. They live their life on Midnight.
Out of step
Nobody has to move at the same time. Every app draws its own line, and they still trade with each other.

Roadmap

One pool, every chain.

The pool lives on Midnight and holds an account on each chain. Moving between chains never leaves it. Information leaks only where money crosses the line, and less crosses it with every chain.

Compliance

Compliance, built in.

  • Deposit rules

    Your contract defines what it accepts, holds or returns.

  • Screening

    Your pool is an ordinary Ethereum account. The tools regulated venues already use work on it unchanged.

  • Segregation

    Each app has its own accounts. Nothing sits in a shared pot with actors you don’t trust.

  • Disclosure

    Define in your contract what users share to take part, and who may ask.
